## Sequence direction for Seedance

Create four original cinematic anime shots in the listed order, totaling 15 seconds in a 16:9 landscape edit. Use the same dark teal local bus throughout the sequence. In heavy rain, it moves away along the center road and blocks the view between Zin Zin on the RIGHT bridge side and Pyae Sone on the LEFT bakery side. Preserve those positions in both wide shots; the bus is nearer the camera in shot 1 and farther away in shot 4. Cut to Zin Zin seen through one rain-beaded bus window; cut to Pyae Sone through the opposite side; end on a high view as the bus departs and their separate routes continue. Neither character makes eye contact or notices the other. Zin Zin keeps the teal plush in her arms and her spotted charm, pink bow and white trinket attached to the brown bag. Pyae Sone keeps his wet white short-sleeve shirt and empty hands, with no extra clothing, umbrella or bag. Maintain one white lighthouse across the bay, the same market, canal footbridge and storm-blue rain. No on-screen words or duplicate characters.
