# Reusable reference asset prompts

All assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool as standalone, full-bleed landscape anime illustrations. The first opening frame established the town; each later place reused earlier images as visual and geographic references. These are production references, not final video frames unless a shot specifically calls for that composition.

## L01 — whole town / S01-01

**References:** S01 concept sheet.  
**Prompt:** A single 16:9 high wide establishing keyframe of a fictional coastal town just after rain at dusk. Rows of houses descend toward a sparkling harbor, one tram on a wet road, a solitary plain white lighthouse at the far right end of a breakwater, sun rays through blue-gray clouds. Luminous original cinematic anime background, stable architecture, no focal people, no text, no borders or contact-sheet layout.

## L02 — hillside tram stop

**References:** L01.  
**Prompt:** A single 16:9 eye-level wide environment view of the same town's small covered tram platform high on the west hillside. Wet rails descend toward the harbor; tiled homes and the same lighthouse appear across the water to the right. Golden dusk after rain, clear spatial layout, stable original anime architecture, no focal people or text.

## L03 — ferry pier

**References:** L01.  
**Prompt:** A single 16:9 eye-level wide view of the same town's lower waterfront ferry terminal, modest white ferry, wet black bollards and railings, hillside houses behind, and the same white lighthouse to the right across the harbor. Dusk after rain, coherent coastal geography, no focal people or text.

## L04 — lighthouse overlook

**References:** L01 and L03.  
**Prompt:** A single 16:9 wide view from the elevated overlook beside the same plain white lighthouse, looking west back across the harbor toward the hillside town and ferry pier. Broad wet terrace, white rail facing town, two separate stair approaches at left and right, lighthouse tower at frame right. Leave room for two people at the rail. Golden dusk after rain, no people or text.

## L05 — covered market and bakery

**References:** L02 and L03.  
**Prompt:** A single 16:9 eye-level view of the same town's mid-slope rain-slick street between tram stop and waterfront. Covered market arcade on one side, warm corner bakery window on the other, tiled roofs, overhead wires, harbor and same white lighthouse glimpsed downhill. Clear walkable space, no focal people or text.

## L06 — canal footbridge

**References:** L03 and L04.  
**Prompt:** A single 16:9 eye-level view of a narrow pedestrian footbridge over a canal, linking the market side at left to the eastern breakwater path at right. Dark railings, wet stone steps, warm harbor lamps, town roofs to left, same plain white lighthouse far right. Space for later near-miss action, no focal people or text.

## P01 — reunion props

**References:** `zinzin.png` for accessory design, `locations/L04-lighthouse-overlook.png` for lighthouse drawing.  
**Prompt:** A single 16:9 clean tabletop prop reference showing, separately and clearly: Zin Zin's teal dinosaur plush with yellow spikes, cream belly, black eyes and pink cheeks; her worn brown shoulder bag with a small golden-cream spotted plush charm, pink bow and white dangling bead; and a cream postcard with only a simple drawing of the town's plain white lighthouse. Keep shapes and colors faithful to the character sheet. No people, extra clothing, text or watermark.

## Shared negative constraints

No extra characters as focal subjects, no new or duplicated lighthouse, no legible signage, no captions, no logo, no watermark, no contact-sheet borders, no letterboxing. Keep the main geography and props recognizable when changing camera angle, weather or time of day.
